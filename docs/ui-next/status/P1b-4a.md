## What the mock does (read, quoted; the lead asked for file:line)

Mock tree c8e44a44, `app/OmniviewNativeLab/frontend/mock/`.

- **Position and shape.** A vertical toolbar of 28 px icon buttons, 4 px apart, in a 40 px column at the LEFT of the body, before the content: `shell-regions.js:314-320` (`toolbarHtml`: `<div class="panel__tools" role="toolbar" aria-orientation="vertical" aria-label="Logs tools" data-roving="col">`), `:309-313` (`toolButton`: `H.ibtn(icon, action, label, 'ghost small', { pressed, disabled, tip })`), `shell.css:125-128` (`.panel__tools { flex: 0 0 40px; flex-direction: column; align-items: center; gap: 4px; padding-block: 4px }`), `shell-panel.css:175-178` (`overflow-y: auto`, bar hidden), ch16 S117 (`research/int-ui/16-ide-shell.md:564`). The body is 1390 px wide at 1440 and the log view is the rest: the kit's `mock-log.json` (tool 1390 x 229) was measured with the column already there.
- **Order, glyphs, names.** `shell-panel.js:2467-2483` (`logsToolbar`): Follow (LuArrowDownToLine, pressed = follow), Pause log updates (LuPause, pressed = paused, disabled `The stream has ended`), Wrap lines, Display options (LuSlidersHorizontal), Jump to time (LuClock), Clear (LuEraser), Filter, Copy (LuCopy, LuCheck while confirming), Export. A toggle keeps one name and says its state in `aria-pressed` (ch16 S117).
- **At the default panel height the mock does not draw them all.** `shell-regions.js:298-308` (`toolSlots`, `slotsFor(height) = floor((height - 4) / 32)`): a body too short keeps the first buttons and puts the rest behind a `⋯` "More actions" button. At 1440 x 900 (body 229 px, 7 slots) the mock shows six buttons and `⋯` (Copy is behind it); at 1280 x 800 (199 px) five and `⋯` (Clear too). Screenshots: `~/Notes/omniview/ui-migration/status/P1b-4a-assets/mock-logs-webkit-1440x900.png`, `mock-logs-webkit-1280x800.png` (and `-body-` crops).
- **Key hints.** The mock gives these buttons none: `toolButton` passes no `keys` (`shell-regions.js:312`; `H.tip({ label, keys, reason })`, `core.js:868-875`), and no chord is bound to follow, pause, clear or copy (`shell-panel.js:2815-2844`; the only chords of the panel are close session `:2796` and find `:2799`). The written spec says so too: "copy, pause, and follow shortcuts are unknown" (`research/int-ui/k8s-req-sessions.md:100`, `k8s-intui-patterns.md:178`). So the tooltips are the name, and for a button that cannot apply the reason under it (`core.js:541`: "the tooltip of a reasoned-disabled icon button is its name, then the reason"). I invented no chord: one needs a registered command (the palette and the commands are a later slice, "every action is also a command", S117).
- **Pause.** `shell-panel.js:1826-1843` (`flushLog`): while paused a line is put in the buffer, not drawn, and counted (`rt.unseen += fresh`); `:2003` the strip says `Paused, N new lines waiting`; `:2033` (`syncLog`) on resume `catchUp` draws them. The kit's feed does the same: the stream holds the lines (bounded, the oldest dropped and counted), the strip counts them (`statText.ts:203-204`), the view draws none. The toolbar only holds and lets go.
- **Clear.** `:2083-2090` (`logClear`): the buffer, the view and the queue are emptied, `dropped: 0`, and a marker row `Cleared. New lines continue below` is the first row.
- **Copy.** `:2063-2070` (`copyLogs`): every line of the view as text, then `confirmCopy` (`:1446-1451`): the button shows LuCheck for 2 s and a polite live region says `N lines copied`; a failure toasts (`:1452`). `lineText` (`:2062`) carries the source label of every line.

## What was built

**Kit** (`packages/kit`): `LogViewModel` gets `clear()` (the view forgets its lines, the selection, the matches, the marker of lines the feed dropped before it read them, and what it had told as dropped; the stream is not touched, the level filter and the query stay, the lines the feed holds that were not read are new lines, a paused feed stays paused, the list goes to the end while it follows and to its top otherwise), `setPaused`, `copyLines` (the selected lines, else every line the list draws, a line each, the source label of each, the clock the rows show; a marker as its own sentence) and `getControls`/`subscribeControls` (five facts: `following`, `drawn`, `held`, `paused`, `feed`; told when one changes and at the feed's own notification, never once a frame). `LineBuffer.clear` and `LineView.reset` serve it. The public surface is one factory and four types: `createLogViewControls()` (`LogViewControls`: a store plus `follow`, `pause`, `clear`, `copy`) and `LogView`'s `controls` prop, which binds the model before the first paint and lets go when the view's effects are released. API report and README updated. Glyphs `pause` (Int UI `run/pause_dark.svg`, the mock's LuPause) and `eraser` (LuEraser) added to the registry (rasterized beside the mock's originals: `pause` 0 differing pixels in WebKit, 0 in Chromium but 4 at 24 px at 2x; `eraser` 15 to 22 in WebKit and 34 to 94 in Chromium, largest channel difference 52, the class of the earlier glyphs).

**Host** (`ui-next/shell/panel/logs/`): `toolbar.ts` (pure: the buttons, names, glyphs, state, reasons, tooltip text, announcements), `clipboard.ts` (the asynchronous clipboard, then the mock's selected-textarea route; never rejects), `LogToolbar.tsx` (the kit's `RovingGroup` column of `IconButton`s, `tip()` tooltips), `LogSession.tsx` (the toolbar, the log view beside it, the session's choices as React state: the clock and the copy confirmation, a polite live region), `LogsBody.tsx`/`.module.css` (the box is now a flex row: the 40 px column and the view). The toolbar lives inside the body's `Activity`, so while the session is not on screen it holds no subscription and no timer. A press toggles from the controls' own snapshot, not from what was last drawn (two presses before React draws again are two toggles: a test).

**Fixture** (`ui-next/plugins/fixture/src/logs.ts`): an eighth session, `quiet` (connected, makes no line: Clear and Copy have nothing to act on), after the twin so the earlier indexes are unchanged.

**Probe** (`ui-next/shell/probes/logs-probe.mjs`): section `toolbar` (below), `hidden` also presses Pause at 10,000 lines a second and counts the commits (one to hold, one to let go: 1 and 1 in Chromium) and arms Copy's clock before the panel is hidden; `fills` now says the body is the column plus the view; `a11y` has a case with a session that has no line; `ASSERTION_OFF=<regex>` drops the failures that match (the repository's convention, so a control can be shown undetected without its assertion). Six controls, none in the shard table yet: `pause-leaks`, `copy-no-source`, `clear-miscount`, `state-lost` (section `toolbar`), `pause-commits`, `toolbar-timer` (section `hidden`).

**Seam** (`ui-next/plugins/e2e/seam.check.mjs`, scenario j): the view-fills-the-body assertion is now column plus view; the one new assertion: Pause pressed, the strip counts the lines that arrive (at least 15 more in 1.5 s at 20 a second), the view draws none, the plugin's listeners stay 3, a press again draws them. ## Decisions for the lead (not chosen silently)

1. **The Local/UTC toggle** is a toolbar toggle in the slot of the mock's Display options (third), glyph `globe`, name `Show timestamps in UTC`. The mock has the choice inside the Display options menu (`shell-panel.js:2666`: Timestamps: Local time, UTC, Hidden); the menu is out of this slice, and the toggle goes when the menu comes.
2. **No key hint** on any button (above). If you want chords they need commands (a later slice).
3. **Copy** copies the selected lines when some are selected, else every line the list draws; the mock's button copies every line (its keyboard copy is one line).
4. **Clear** writes no marker row (the mock writes `Cleared. New lines continue below`); the dropped count starts again (the mock's does too), and the lines the feed held but the view had not read are shown after it (the mock empties its queue). A paused feed's waiting lines stay waiting.
5. **`role="group"`, not `role="toolbar"`**: the kit's `RovingGroup` is a named group (the panel's own actions are one). Changing it is one prop in a kit part I do not own.
6. **A short panel scrolls the column** (no bar, the focus scrolls into view: `shell-panel.css:176-178`) where the mock's script shows the first buttons and a `⋯` menu (`shell-regions.js:298-320`). Five buttons fit from a body of 164 px (a panel of 205 px). The `⋯` needs a menu part.
7. **Pause is unavailable for a stream that failed** as well as for one that ended (`The stream has failed`); the mock disables it for an ended one only.
8. **Outside my list, done minimally and listed**: two glyphs in the Icon registry; the model additions in `packages/kit/src/log/**` (Clear cannot be done from outside: a feed swap would reset the level filter, which lives in the model); `bench/log/` (`--toolbar`); `host-fixture.tsx` (a comment). I told Main before doing them and had no answer.
9. **Probe budget: the shard table has no room.** `scripts/ci/probe-shards.mjs` holds 3,387 s of units in 8 shards of at most 432 s: 69 s spare in all. The new section and six controls cost more than that (about 130 s on a runner, estimated from 56 s for the section at load 60 to 90 and 10 to 15 s a control, none measured on a runner). I did not touch the table: a seventh shard's worth of work needs a ninth shard (a change to `.github/workflows/ui-next-kit-probes.yml`, which makes the PR a whole-suite PR, rule 32), a repack, or fewer controls. Your call.
10. **State is per session and not persisted.** The clock choice is React state of the session's body, which the panel keeps mounted for the session's life (tool and tab switches keep it); Follow is the view's, Pause the stream's. A restored session starts as a new one (its lines are gone too).

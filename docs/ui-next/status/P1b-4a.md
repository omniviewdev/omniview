## Decisions for the lead (not chosen silently)

1. **The Local/UTC toggle** is a toolbar toggle in the slot of the mock's Display options (third), glyph `globe`, name `Show timestamps in UTC`. The mock has the choice inside the Display options menu (`shell-panel.js:2666`: Timestamps: Local time, UTC, Hidden). Accepted by the lead for this slice; **it moves INTO the Display options menu when that slice is built** (the toggle and its More-menu item go with it).
2. **No key hint** on any button (above). If you want chords they need commands (a later slice).
3. **Copy** writes every line the list draws, as the mock's button does (`shell-panel.js:2063-2070`, `copyLogs`: the lines of its view with the level filter applied; a selection plays no part there; the keyboard's ⌘C is the active line, `:3091-3096`). The selected lines are the list's own `copy` event, the keyboard's copy (kit, unchanged). The polite region says `N lines copied` with the number written; the mock says it with the count of ALL its lines even when a level filter wrote fewer (`rt.lineCount`, `:2067`): a mock quirk, not copied.
4. **Clear follows the mock.** The mock (`logClear`, `shell-panel.js:2083-2090`): `dropped: 0, lineCount: 0`, the buffer, the view and the queue emptied, then the marker row `Cleared. New lines continue below`; the strip then says `0 lines · … · Following` with no dropped part (measured in S1). Ours forgets everything made so far: the lines the view holds, its selection, matches, drop marker and counts (so the strip claims no line was "dropped" that the user cleared), AND what the feed holds that the view had not read (the lines that came while the feed was paused, the lines of the last frame, a drop it had not told: read and thrown away, as the mock empties its queue and sets its count to 0); a paused feed stays paused and holds only the lines made after the Clear; the level filter and the query stay. No marker row (the mock writes one and later copies it as a line: a deviation, and a mock quirk). Tests say each part (`does not tell again the lines the feed dropped before the clear`, `forgets a drop the feed made before the clear that the view had not heard of`, `leaves a paused feed paused and clears the lines it held`). The first version kept the lines a paused feed held and told a pending drop, which contradicted the mock and the State line; CodeRabbit's review of the first push said so, and the lead had ruled to follow the mock.
5. **`role="toolbar"` with `aria-orientation="vertical"` and the name `Logs tools`**, as the mock's (`shell-regions.js:319`). The kit's `RovingGroup` got an optional `role: 'group' | 'toolbar'` (default group; a toolbar says `aria-orientation` from its `kind`; any other value is a group). I did not need `group`: no axe rule or nesting conflict asked for it (axe ran on the page: no `aria-allowed-*` finding).
6. **A short panel: the mock's rule, built with the kit's menu.** `shell-regions.js:298-308`: `slotsFor(height) = floor((height - 4) / 32)`; with more buttons than slots the FIRST `slots - 1` stay and the REST (the LAST buttons) go behind a `⋯` "More actions" button (tooltip `More actions`, `aria-haspopup`, `shell-regions.js:318`), whose menu ("Logs actions") lists the hidden buttons (checkbox items for the toggles). The kit HAS a menu part, `RowMenu` with `createRowMenu` (the page header's overflow uses it), so the `⋯` is built with it, in `LogToolbar.tsx`. The trigger is the kit's `IconButton` with a new optional `popup="menu"` prop, not `RowMenuTrigger` (a 24 px row button that opens on ArrowUp and ArrowDown, which a vertical toolbar needs for moving). `RowMenuItem` has no `checked`, so an item says the action: Stop following or Follow, Pause log updates or Resume log updates, Show timestamps in UTC or in local time. At the default panel (bodies of 229 and 199 px: 7 and 6 slots) all five buttons fit and no `⋯` is drawn; at the panel's least (120 px, a body of 79 px, 2 slots) Follow and `⋯` stay, at 200 px three and `⋯` (the `⋯` stands where the mock's measured one does: `[6,36,28,28]`, `[6,100,28,28]`). The column no longer scrolls (the `overflow-y: auto` of the mock's CSS stays as its safety net).

## The three bounds, in one line

**Packed to at most 432 s listed; a shard fails above 614 s real; a unit fails above listed x 1.25 or listed + 60 s, whichever is more** (the larger of the two margins, not their sum: a 20 s unit fails above 80 s, a 400 s unit above 500 s). 432 s is the planning limit (the 9-minute wall less the 90th percentile of the setup) and is not raised; 614 s is derived below; the shard job times out at 15 minutes.

## 1. What was tried (controls by change), what the review found, why it was dropped

The first version (commit `8e71e03f`) ran every probe on every pull request and, of the 15 control units (1,664 s), the ones whose declared paths the pull request's changed files reached (`probe-select.mjs`, a glob matcher; each unit listed `paths`); everything it could not classify ran every control (a push to main, a nightly run, a re-run, the shared harness, `scripts/ci/**`, the kit's tokens). Replayed on 13 merged pull requests: #645 ran 1 control unit of 15 and skipped 1,628 s, #650 ran 4 and skipped 1,149 s, a documentation pull request ran none, and the ones that touched the probe workflow, `scripts/ci/**` or the shared harness ran all 15.

The independent review (ReviewProbes656, 2026-11-22, verdict FIX FIRST; every number below is a command it ran) found:

- **B1, a blocker.** A 12-line change to one kit file that no unit lists (`TooltipHost/controller.ts`: text that is cut tells its whole name whether or not its markup says so) leaves the tour probe green and the kit's tests green (90 passed), makes the control `names-no-tip` unable to fail (`NOT DETECTED`), and the selector ran 0 of 15 control units. The first red would be the push to main, which nothing reads: the merge gate reads the pull request's own rollup, GitHub mails whoever pushed, there is no bot. On main's scheme the same pull request is red on the pull request. The history was on the author's side on how often: in 273 runs of this workflow since 2026-11-16 no control ever failed with its probe green (37 failed jobs in 33 failing attempts: 26 probe, 6 infrastructure, 5 table check, 0 control only, 0 drift). It is rare, and silent when it comes.
- **S1.** Three controls name an attribute (`data-ov-clip`, `data-ov-lane`) that files outside their globs write.
- **S3.** Nothing pinned the selection data: of 94 narrowings of a glob 58 were noticed by no test, and of 115 removals of a control 31.
- **S7.** What it saved, replayed on the 69 first-parent commits of main since 2026-11-16 that start this workflow, the table edits rewritten to unit-file edits: 41 (59 percent) are still full runs (the workflow, a script, the harness, a kit-wide path); the mean planned saving is 9 percent (17 percent for the 13 that edit no other CI file; 6 percent as merged); and a full run costs more than the old one (13 jobs and 5,714 s measured against 11 and 5,266).

**Decision (the lead's, final): by-change selection of the negative controls is removed.** The saving is small and a fault can merge silently for it. Removed: `probe-select.mjs`, its tests and data (`probe-select.prs.json`), the `paths` of every unit, the nightly run, the dispatch input `changed_paths`, the selection in the plan, its notices in the summaries, the dependency of the engine on `changes.mjs`. 1. **The unit directory.** `scripts/ci/probe-units/<probe>.mjs` exports `units`: the unit that runs the probe and the units that run its controls, each `{ id, name, script, env?, controls?, seconds }`. No shard, no path (`check` refuses a unit that declares `paths`). A pull request that adds a probe adds a file; the seconds a drift message asks for live in the unit's own file.
2. **Packing at run time** (`probe-pack.mjs`, exact search where it can be, first fit decreasing where it cannot; the same answer for the same units in any order). The plan on this table: **11 shards of 407, 406, 405, 405, 404, 404, 403, 398, 397, 396 and 386 s listed** (4,411 s over 432 is 10.2: eleven is the fewest). `LIMIT_SECONDS` = 540 - 108 = 432, the value on main: not raised. The plan job fails when it would plan no shard (an empty matrix is never written), and when the table cannot be read: `plan`, `run` and `verify` all stop on a unit file that does not load (a mutant that let `plan` go on with 19 probes survived the first version's tests).
3. **The splits keep the sets.** `node controls-diff.mjs` (a copy is in `N25/`) against the table of `origin/main` at `7471b52f`: probes 20 and 20, controls 115 and 115, **both difference sets empty**, no duplicate, the same environment for all 135 runs, the controls of each probe in the same order; only the step names of the 36 split runs changed. The three splits: the panel probe's 480 s unit (three units: 215, 160 and 105 s), the toast layer's controls (384 s: 214 and 170) and the Logs probe's (382 s: 170 and 212).
4. **Drift is enforced for each unit and for each shard.** After a shard ran, a unit that took more than its listed seconds plus the larger of a quarter of them and 60 s fails the shard with the number to put in its file (faster by the same margin only warns); and the shard fails when all its units add up to more than the ceiling (a shard whose units are each just inside their margin can take 866 s: the review's `drift-bounds.mjs`). Both are tested at their boundaries and replayed on the real runs.
5. **The verdict job `Kit probes` runs `always()`** (a cancelled run leaves a check, not an absence). First it requires the plan and every shard to have succeeded; then it downloads what each shard wrote (`ran-<shard>-probe.json`: the units it ran, whether each passed, its controls) and compares it with the table of its own checkout: **every unit and every control of it ran once and passed, in as many shards as the plan made.** It fails on any missing unit, any missing shard, a shard that reported twice, a unit that ran twice or lacked a control, and on a plan whose number of shards is empty or unreadable. It lists what it required (`20 probes and 115 controls, in 35 units, ran once and passed, in 11 shards`) in its job summary. The report upload overwrites by name, so that a re-run of a shard (or of the run) replaces its report.
6. **The manifest is the floor.** `scripts/ci/probe-units.manifest.json` lists every probe script and every control CI proves (the 20 probes and 115 controls; it equals the table of `origin/main` exactly, compared without the unit files). `check` fails when it lists a probe or a control that no unit runs, so a pull request that drops one edits that file, where a reviewer reads it; a control may move to another unit of its probe. **It is a floor, not a mirror:** a probe or control that it does not list is not an error, so two pull requests that add probes do not both edit it (and a probe added without a manifest entry can be dropped later without a diff in it: add yours). The test holds at least the 20 probes and 115 controls, and takes every control and every unit of the real table out in turn: 150 of 150 fail the check.

What I found when I verified (it was not as reported): the engine's test file imported the deleted `probe-select.mjs` and could not load, so the first step of every shard (`node --test scripts/ci/probe-*.test.mjs`) would have failed: the tests are rewritten, 107, test first; the ceiling of 600 s was picked, now derived; `verify` said "35 units" and now says what ran; a re-run of a shard would have uploaded the same artifact name again and failed or left the first attempt's report, now `overwrite: true`; the shard job's timeout (20 minutes) was not tied to the ceiling, now 15; and "a quarter and 60 s" read as a sum, which it is not (the larger of the two): the line above and the comments say so.

## 4. The centre-open bound

**What failed.** The notices probe's `centre open` read, on the runner, a median of 38 ms (1440 x 900) and 35 ms (1280 x 800) in WebKit against a bound of 33.3 ms (two frames) in the first attempt of run 44932365922 (#650); attempt 2, same code, passed.

## The probe

`packages/kit/probes/form-probe.mjs` drives the real `ui-next` parts page in WebKit and Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 1280 x 800, and judges the six parts in six sections. 1,652 checks, 0 failures; 31 s on a laptop (parity 20 s, axe 2 s, themes 1.6 s, secret 1.2 s, keyboard 0.4 s).

- **parity**: 27 cases of `form-cases.mjs` against the mock, in Void and in Light. The mock side is the mock's own helpers (`H.field`, `H.switch`, `H.stepper`, `H.select`, and the markup of its secret) staged in a neutral box and measured by `mock-form-measure.mjs` in each engine and theme into `mock-form.json` (the reference; run it with the mock served as under "Dev tools"). A case is measured at rest and in the states it names (rest, hover, focus: real focus and the real pointer, by `probe-lib.mjs`) and by parts: the rectangle of a part inside its root (x and width within 1 px, y and height within 0.5 px) and its colour, background, border, radius, padding, font and outline (exact strings), and the `::after` of a switch's track. Cases: six faces of a text input (rest, mono, invalid, warning, read-only, unavailable), the label with its required mark and its aside, the aligned tracks, the lane in each of seven kinds, the switch in four states, the number field in three, the select in two, the secret in two.
  The root of each face and select case is also read as a part (`face`) for its font role, code or interface face, which `summarize` does not hold: a code-face field drawn in the interface face fails `parity/face-mono/face: font`.
- **states**: every example in every state of the field model, read from the page: labelled, invalid, warning, required, unavailable for a reason (focusable, `aria-disabled`, described by the reason), read-only, the words of a secret, what a stored secret offers (Replace and nothing that reveals it), the stepper's buttons out of the tab order.
- **secret**: a secret typed with real key presses is in the live input's `value` and nowhere else in the page: no attribute, no text node, no markup, no storage, not the title, and no other field's live `value` (every field is read, and only the one being typed into is exempt while it is typed into); while typed, shown, hidden again, and typed after Replace. After Escape every field is empty, with no exemption. A stored secret has no value in the page at all.
- **keyboard**: Escape on a draft, the arrows and Page Up of the number field, Tab leaving it without stopping on a step, Space and Enter on a switch, a read-only switch focusable and inert, a locked select (focus checked first) that a letter key does not change and whose keydown is prevented (a controlled select is put back by the page, so the value alone would not say whether the key was refused), while the same key does change an unlocked select and is not prevented there, so that the check can fail (a letter selects the option that starts with it, in every engine), Enter on Retry, and no write to the root by any part.
- **themes**: 21 paints of the parts (borders, fills, text, lane colours, the switch's track) beside the token each names, in all nine themes: nothing is a colour of its own.
- **axe**: axe-core over the six parts, in Void and in Light, serious and critical violations fail (colour contrast is judged here; jsdom cannot).

## Decisions and deviations (for the lead)

1. **`Field` owns `disabledReason` and `readOnly` and prints the reason in its lane** (accepted). The mock's `H.field` has neither; its `H.settingRow` does (`core.js:662`), and the model's `lock.reason` is also the lane's message, so one prop locks the control and says why. The controls take the same props for use without a Field.
2. **`SecretInput` words are the mock's** (`shell-page-settings.js:725-728`): "Saved · hidden" for a stored secret, "Not set" for none. The one word that is not in the mock is "Changed" (a typed value the model holds and the page never shows). The input is always there; it never receives the stored secret, it starts empty and holds only what the user types, and Replace unlocks it, so the label's `for`, the focus and the DOM never change under the user. The mock's disabled Show for a stored secret ("A saved secret is never shown again.") is not drawn: no control that cannot act.
3. **A read-only switch and a read-only select follow the mock**: painted and exposed as unavailable (`aria-disabled`, no `aria-readonly`), focusable, and described by the lane or by "Read-only.". The mock's `lockControls` (`core.js:188`) does this for every control but a text field. The select is my extension of the ruling (the lead ruled on the switch); text, number and secret fields stay `readonly` (F-4).
4. **The number field is the mock's own 6 characters (46.8 px), not 180 px.** My first status said the 180 was "the default width of its input"; staging the same markup inside and outside `.setting-row__control` shows it is the N10 leak (`min-width: 180px` on `.inp`): 46.8 px outside, 180 px inside. For the select the lead ruled the spec over the leak; I did the same here. If the lead prefers the settings form's 260 px group, it is one rule in `NumberInput.module.css` and one assertion.
5. **Erratum N10 (for the lead):** `cat-globalb.js:14` injects `.setting-row__control :is(select,.inp){min-width:180px}` into every page. It makes the settings select 180 px although `shell-page-settings.css:48` says 200 px, and the settings stepper's field 180 px instead of 6ch. The references are measured outside `.setting-row__control`.
6. **The stepper's two buttons are not tab stops** (one stop per number, `14:109` P6, the mock's `oneStop`): an exception to decision A14 for a composite widget, to be recorded beside A14's existing exception in `decisions.md` (the lead's file). Decrease and Increase are pointer shortcuts and the arrows are the keyboard's.
7. **Not built:** the mock's collapse of `.field` to one track below 760 px of VIEWPORT (`base.css:2077`): a part must not answer to the window; the form layout step should do it by container. `labelHidden`, multiline text, the Settings row (title, description, reset slot, the lane's 20 px gutter): the form layout.

Whoever merges second (`ui-next/o1b2-notice-centre` adds six) re-derives the names list and the budget from the real total and regenerates the API report.

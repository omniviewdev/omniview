## Findings and what each was decided by

### D4: a lone tab is cut in the middle (no change: it is the spec; the probe pins it)

- **What the mock says** (`aws-wails-native-lab/app/OmniviewNativeLab/frontend/mock`): `shell-tabs.css`: `.tabstrip .tab-item { flex: 0 1 208px; width: 208px; }` with `min-width: 96px; max-width: 208px`, and its comment: "Every face starts at the same 208px basis, regardless of its content. Equal shrink factors divide the available scroller width after gaps and the pinned separator; the explicit 96px floor then makes the scroller overflow. The strip and scroller keep their content-sized upper bound, so at the 208px plateau the + stays beside the last tab and spare room belongs to the drag lane." `shell-tabs.js` `splitTitle`: "Middle elision of the title (S17, ch 12 §7): an unbroken head with an end ellipsis and a terminal suffix that keeps its end": the head ends in an ellipsis, the tail keeps its end and loses its start.
- **What the design says** (`research/int-ui/16-ide-shell.md`): S17: "Width follows the equal responsive policy in S17a, **not title length**. Inside that width the scope elides first, then the title in the middle (ch 12 §7)... Preserve full identity despite visual truncation." S17a: "All movable/addable document tabs in one pane's strip use the same width... clamped to 96–208px. **Small counts stop growing at 208px; remaining space belongs to the native drag lane.**" The changelog (line 937): "Owner-directed responsive document tabs. A7, S4, S17 and S53 now use equal 96–208px widths per movable strip... **The prior intrinsic-title/never-compress policy... superseded.**" ch12 §7: "When space is constrained, the prefix uses end ellipsis and an oversized suffix retains its end."
- **What was measured** (the mock's own `H.tabStrip`, both engines, both viewports, `mock-strip-text.json`): a lone `payment-gateway · Deployment` tab is a 208 x 28 face; its text has 148 px (the face less 8, 16, 4 and the close control's 24 on the shell's side; the mock's 7 + 31, its border and the same 16 and 4); the head is 91.84 px for 113 px of text (cut, an ellipsis), the tail 56.16 px for 69.38 (its first 13 px lost) in the normalized font, and with the mock's own Inter 92.3 and 55.7 px for 122 and 73.9: `payment-gat…ployment`, the demo's text. The shell reads the same to 1 px in all eleven cases of the strip (`titles` and `twins` sections of `tour-probe.mjs`).
- **Decision: no change to the shell.** The demo's expectation ("a tab takes the room it needs up to the max width and truncates only when the strip is full") is the policy the owner superseded. A title that does not fit 148 px is cut however empty the strip is, in the mock as here, and the tab's tooltip carries the whole identity (`Plugin › Scope › Path`, S17). The one `{name} · {Kind}` that keeps its Kind while its name is cut is the mock's rule ("a terminal suffix that keeps its end"). **If the owner wants a lone tab to grow with its title, that is an amendment of S17a and A7 and an erratum of the mock (row N10), after which the shell follows; it is not a defect of the shell.** Asked of the lead in the result of this step.
- **What was added**: the `titles` section (face 208 px for a lone tab, 96 px at the least with twelve, one part up to twelve graphemes, head and tail beyond, every part where the mock's is and cut as the mock's is: 1 px) and its control `title-cut`; the picture `S3-assets/strip-one-tab-webkit-void-1440x900.png`.

### D5: the sidebar's open groups are lost when its plugin stops and comes back (fixed)

- **Cause**: the open groups were the state of the `Tree` component (`useState`), and the tree is drawn only while its plugin runs (`registrationOf`: no sidebar for a plugin that is not `ready`; the shell draws no sidebar region at all for a plugin that stopped (PU1: the whole left island vanishes) and an empty one while it loads). A tree that came back started again from the registration's `open` (none) and the ancestors of the page's entry. Nothing in `docs/**` named it: `S3a.md` section 3 lists "the sidebar's open groups (a component's state, not a model's)" as not restored on a session restore, which is about a restart, and this is a plugin's restart.
- **Fix** (`core/tree.ts` `TreeMemory`, `Tree.tsx`, `Sidebar.tsx`, one line in `Shell.tsx`): the shell holds one memory for the window's life (`useState(createTreeMemory)`); a tree keeps a record there while it is on screen and recalls it when it mounts, under the key it already has as an element (`owner` and `label`: what was opened in one entry's sidebar stays with that entry, even when another is called the same and has the same ids). The record is the open set (a group the nodes do not hold yet is kept: the skeleton comes before the plugin's data) and the entry the tree last made sure was in view, so a plugin that comes back **on the same entry opens nothing the user closed**, and one that comes back on another entry opens that entry's groups, once, as before. A rail that goes to another plugin's sidebar and returns shows the first as it was left, which the code's own comment already promised.
- **What session restore does** (unchanged, said here as asked): the groups are not saved (`S3a.md` section 3, README "Not saved, on purpose"); after a restart or a reload of the page the sidebar starts with the registration's groups and the ancestors of the page on screen. The memory lives as long as the window, not longer.
- **Test first**: `core/tree.test.ts` (3), `Tree.test.tsx` (8 for the memory) and `Shell.test.tsx` (4, red by behaviour at `1ab0fcc4`: "expected aria-expanded true" and "expected the closed group not to open"); the probe's `memory` section on the host fixture (`?groups=2`) fails in Chromium without the one line in `Shell.tsx` with the demo's symptom (`{"group":"true","second":"false"}`: the page's group open and the group the user opened closed) and passes with it in both engines at both sizes; **seam scenario n** (the real Go host, a real killed process) gains the sidebar assertion: the user closes the page's group before the kill and it is still closed when the plugin is back, and the plugin's hook is told that no group is open; its control `sidebar-reset` is detected, and with `SEAM_REMOVE=sidebar-reset` passes undetected.
- **Picture**: `S3-assets/sidebar-after-restart-fixture-webkit-void-1440x900.png` (host fixture: Second group open with its two entries, Group closed) and `S3-assets/sidebar-after-restart-seam-webkit-void-1440x900.png` (the real host after the kill).

### D8: the tab strip shows no connection name (the mock's rule is already the shell's; one difference found and fixed)

- **What the mock says** (`shell-tabs.js`, the header of `H.tabStrip`): "The face carries NO scope by default (S17, amended in wave 4): the muted 12px text after the title is a DISAMBIGUATOR. It shows only on tabs that would otherwise read the same (the same icon and title)... and then it is the shortest text that tells them apart (D8): the plugin's own `facets` first (namespace, then context for a Kubernetes object), then the scope, else the parent crumb of the path, else the plugin name... Singleton and host tabs are unique by construction and never get one; two tabs identical in every facet get none. The full scope stays in the tab's name and tooltip." It is the rule of the panel's session tabs (A22), and it is in a second text on the same line, not a second line or a lane.
- **So the demo's expectation ("the tab strip shows connection names") is not the mock's**: a window with one connection has no twins, and a lone `Nodes` tab says no scope; the name is in its accessible name, its tooltip, the status bar's path and the lane (`kind-demo`), as the mock has it. The model already derives it (`core/tabs/identity.ts`: `qualify`, `hostFacets`: the scope's label, which is the connection's NAME, never its key; C1g), and `Tab.tsx` draws it. Two connections in one window cannot be reached from the UI (the demo says so); they come from a deep link or a restored session, and `PluginHost.names.test.tsx` and `PluginHost.detail.test.tsx` hold the host's half.
- **Added**: `TabStrip.test.tsx` (4: two tabs of one title on two connections whose keys are in no text, name or tip and whose names are drawn; a lone tab draws none and its name and tip carry the connection; a third tab says none; closing a twin takes the other's off), mutation-checked by hand (the facet reading the scope's id instead of its label: 3 tests red; no qualifier drawn: 4 red); the `twins` section against the mock's own twin tabs, which **found a real difference**: the qualifier's floor of five characters (`min-inline-size: min(100%, 5ch)` from a content width of 64 px) was measured in the face's 13 px type, 36.14 px for `north` against the mock's 33.36 (its `.tab__scope` is 12 px), which took 2.8 px from a twin's title (64 red lines before the fix, both engines, both sizes); the qualifier is 12 px now (`TabStrip.module.css`). Controls `twin-lone` and `twin-hidden`; the picture `S3-assets/strip-twins-webkit-void-1440x900.png`.

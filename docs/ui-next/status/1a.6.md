## Decisions and findings for the lead

- **Refresh's reason while the first load runs** is `Loading`, not the mock's `Refreshing` (which would be false then); `Refreshing` is for a refresh in flight.
- **`Updated` stays 8 s** (the presenter's calm-outcome clock) unless dismissed or the next Refresh replaces it.
- **No connections** is drawn as the error notice (`Couldn't load Pods` / `No connections`, no Retry); the real disconnected and failure states are 1a.7.
- **Failure beside rows** is kept as `failed` in the source and the page keeps listing; the footer wording `Load failed`/`Access denied` with Retry is 1a.8.
- **Age tip** is read when the cell mounts; the label is the live one.
- **Names are plain text**, not links (plan 1.2: nothing to open before the detail slice).
- The registration still names the connection from the URL (`?connection=`), as in 1a.5; the connection the page picked and the sidebar count reach the shell in 1a.7.
- E6 (hidden-columns note), E9 (Clear filters in the toolbar) and E10 (`N Pods loaded` while the set arrives) are followed as proposed in the plan.
- Tooltips: icon-only controls and clipped text carry `data-ov-tip` and `data-ov-clip` (PR #519, not merged); nothing shows until it lands.

# T6 panes: focus by keyboard and the group after a reload (#697 step 6, the last part)

Branch `feat/panel-panes-focus-persist` from `origin/main` `477e56e76` (the model #806, the layout #814). The editor area (step 7) is blocked and not here.

## Decisions

1. **The chords** (`core/chords.ts` `paneChordOf`, wired in `panel/usePanelChords.ts`, only while the keyboard is in the panel, like Close session): on a **Mac** Command Option with the arrows (left, right, up, down) and with `[` and `]` (previous and next pane), VS Code's; on **Windows and Linux** Control Shift with Left and Right and with `[` and `]`. The reasons are the terminal's own rules (`panel/terminal/keys.ts`, S52): a terminal keeps every Control chord on a Mac and every Control chord without Shift elsewhere, and Alt and the arrows are the shell's word movement (ESC b and ESC f), so VS Code's Alt chords of Windows and Linux are not taken; Command chords and Control Shift chords are the window's (`keyOf` answers `app`, and a test pins that every pane chord does). Control Shift Up and Down are the command-output selection (#694), so a platform without Command has no up and down; in a COLUMN of panes left and right are the previous and the next pane there. No conflict with F6 (`leave`), find, copy, paste or the command jumps.
   *Not reachable:* in a browser on a Mac, Command Option and the arrows are the browser's tab switch, and the web alternates of the shell (Control Option) are the terminal's: there the panes are reached by a click, by Tab (the document is in the screen's order) and by the strip of session tabs. A palette command was left out: it would change every list of the palette's commands that the existing tests enumerate, for a keyboard path that the same person has in the tab strip.
2. **Where the keyboard goes** (`paneToward`, pure, in `core/panel/panes.ts`): next and previous wrap round; left and right (row) or up and down (column) stop at the ends and are nothing in the other axis; one pane, or an active pane that is not among them, has nowhere to go and the key is left alone (not cancelled). `moveToPane` (`panel/focus.ts`) makes the pane the active one in the model and puts the keyboard in its emulator's input (else the first control it has).
3. **The screen reader:** the emulator's input is named `Terminal input, pane 2 of 3, <title>` in a group (a screen reader says an input's name when the keyboard arrives in it), and `Terminal input, <title>` when the terminal is alone, as before. The body gets its seat from a context the deck provides (`PaneSeatProvider`); the provider is ALWAYS there (null for a tool with one session), because a body whose tree gains a wrapper when its tool is split would be remounted, and its emulator with it (`PaneEmulators.test.tsx` caught the first draft).
4. **Persistence.** The window's saved session already carried the group: `serialiseSession` writes `serialise(panel)`, whose `layout` #806 added, and `restorePanel` reads it back, so the saved shares, the order and the active pane were in the document before this change. What was missing was proof and the gone terminal. Proof: `session/session.test.ts` (the group written with the burst, restored, and a drag of a sash written again), `terminals.test.ts` (each pane's terminal attached again: the host's list gives each saved session its feed by its id, the group untouched) and the probe's `panes.reload` in a real emulator.
5. **A terminal that is gone leaves its pane** (`leavePane` in the model: the session stays a tab, its share goes to the others in proportion, the focus passes as when a pane closes, a group of one is dissolved; `leaveGonePanes` in `terminals.ts`). It runs when the host has ANSWERED (`openFound`) and a saved terminal in a pane got no feed. A host that could not answer is not an answer (the saved tabs stay as they were), and a terminal the host lists as ended keeps its pane, whose lane offers Restart. Its tab stays, ended, as the tab of a gone terminal always did.

## What is proved, and how

Tests first, seen red (`test(terminal): pane chords, the pane the keyboard goes to, a pane that leaves, the seat in the input's name, the group after a reload, red`): chords (`core/chords.test.ts`), `paneToward` and `leavePane` and the fuzz's `leave` operation (`core/panel/panes.*.test.ts`), the page tests (`panel/panes/Panes.test.tsx`: the invented stream bodies with a text input, **no emulator**), the input's name (`TerminalSplit.test.tsx`: the real registry and body with a **FAKE emulator**), the registry's reload (`terminals.test.ts`, fake emulator and fake feeds), the session (`session.test.ts`). Three of the persistence tests (the group in the session, the group back with its terminals attached, the ended terminal's pane kept) were green when written, for the reason in decision 4, and say so.

The **real emulator** (the product's own xterm, DOM renderer) is the probe's (`terminal-panes.mjs`, `PART=panes`), in WebKit and Chromium at both viewports, against the probe's own host (which can vanish and end a terminal while the page is away): `panes.keys-focus` and `panes.chords-quiet` (the chords, the stops and wraps, the input's name, the selected tab, and no byte to a shell); `panes.reload` (three panes of uneven shares and the middle one active, `about:blank` and back: the same sessions in the same order, the same widths within 2 px, the same active pane, three terminals attached again, none made); `panes.gone` (one terminal vanished while away: two panes, in the proportion they had, its tab stays ended); `panes.ended` (one ended: its pane stays with Restart). Five controls of the probe's table, in both engines, both halves.

Code controls: `docs/ui-next/status/T6-panes-focus/controls.sh.txt` (the faults in the real files, put back after each).

**The seam.** `r3` (the reload of the real host) was not given a group: a group in `r3` means a Split in the real host, two real shells and the counts of tabs that the checks after it (`1d` to `1f`) rely on, and each run of the scenario costs minutes. What it gains cheaply is one assertion: the window's saved panel holds no `layout` while the terminals are tabs of one body (a group writes one, and only a group does), so the document a window without panes saves is the one it always saved. The group's own reload is proved against the protocol-faithful probe host with a real emulator, and the document by the session test.

## Not verified

- A native WKWebView window; the nine themes (no colour is added).
- The chords on a Mac keyboard (the probe's engines say Linux, so the Control Shift pair is what ran; the Command Option pair is unit-tested through `paneChordOf` and `keyOf`).
- The reload of a group against the REAL host (the seam's r3 holds no group).
- Stacked panes in a browser: `paneToward` and the chords handle a column (unit tests); no probe starts one.

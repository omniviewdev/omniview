## 4. The drift guard

`driftWarnings(id, results, measured)` in `scripts/ci/probe-shards.mjs`, called by `run` after the summary: one `::warning title=Kit probe unit seconds (shard N)::<unit> took 127 s on this runner, 100 s in MEASURED_SECONDS (27 percent over; ...)` for each unit that took more than `DRIFT_RATIO` (25 %) over its value **and** more than `DRIFT_FLOOR_SECONDS` (10 s) more. A unit that has no value is named too. A unit with a run that failed is left out (a probe that stopped early or hung says nothing of its cost). It never fails the shard.

The floor is mine, not the ticket's. On the 186 unit runs of the 8 runs, 3 are over 25 % (the strip's controls at 38 and 36 s for 28, the palette's controls at 81 s for 63): two of them are 8 and 10 s over, noise on a unit of 28 s. With the floor 1 of 186 warns (the palette's controls at +18 s: one run of the 8); without it 3 do, in 3 runs of the 8. A warning that fires on three runs in eight is one nobody reads.

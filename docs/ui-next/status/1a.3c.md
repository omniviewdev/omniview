## (3) The row menu in WebKit

What 1a.3b saw: after Tab inside the open menu "1 root, and the steps after it fail" (5 failures, Chromium none). Reproduced, then taken apart in a real engine:

1. **Tab and Shift+Tab are not the defect.** With focus in the menu (opened by key or by pointer) they close it and focus goes on from the trigger (in WebKit to the table region, because WebKit's Tab skips buttons; in Chromium to the neighbouring trigger). The open menu that failed the probe was the 5,025-row example's, left by the probe's own `clipping()` step, which moves the focused popup in the document.
2. **Real defect A (what that step exposed): WebKit sends no blur when the focused node is removed or moved** (events: none in WebKit, `blur` and `focusout` in Chromium). Focus drops on the body, the menu stays open, no key reaches it, and Escape and Tab close nothing. In the product the same happens when the focused item goes away (its row's items changed). Fix: the handle's one MutationObserver, which already watched for the trigger leaving the page, puts focus back on the popup when focus is on the body while the menu is open. No listener was added (open still adds exactly a press, a scroll and a resize on the window).
3. **Real defect B (hidden under the same failures): in WebKit the open trigger could not close its menu.** WebKit gives a button no focus on a press and moves focus to the nearest focusable ancestor, the table region; the menu closed on that focus move and the click then opened it again. Fix: a press on a trigger moves no focus (`onMouseDown` prevents the default); the click decides.

Test first: three tests failed in jsdom (which, like WebKit, sends no blur on removal) and now pass, with one for Tab and Shift+Tab from a pointer-opened menu that passed from the start. The probe (`row-menu-probe.mjs`) gained: no menu left open by an earlier step, Tab and Shift+Tab from a pointer-opened menu, and focus dropped by a moved popup; against the old code it fails with 6 failures in WebKit, each naming its step. The mock's contract (`mock/shell-menu.js` `onKey`, research chapter 05 "Interaction": the Tab row; chapter 12 only says "menu behaviour is `H.menu`"): Tab closes the menu, restores focus to the trigger and does not cancel the key; Escape closes one level wherever focus is. Not built, as in 1a.3b: PageUp and PageDown (the mock: 10 rows), type-ahead, the popup shadow and the 8 px margin. The mock's Escape reaches the menu wherever focus is because its keydown listener is on the window for good; the kit keeps its three listeners, so Escape reaches the menu where focus is in it, which the rescue now guarantees for a focus lost by the page.

7. The table probe ran in the Linux image here and has not run on a GitHub runner: its first run there is this PR's `Kit probes` job.

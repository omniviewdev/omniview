## Verdict, plainly

1. **The image does not make the median slower: it makes it faster, by about 24 s per shard** (setup, which is the job minus the probes: median 80 s with the image against 104 s without, n = 15 shard jobs each). "Initialize containers" is 32 s median (24 to 46) and replaces about 57 s median (59 mean) of Node, pnpm, Task, browser cache and browser install (46 s median for the install alone). I1's estimate (about -18 s) held; its fear (+20 s) did not.
2. **The wall time of the job does not move**: the slowest shard is the panel probe with its controls (about 600 s of probes) in both, and its run-to-run spread (probes 589 to 647 s in the three image runs, 590 to 609 s in the three without) is larger than the 15 s the image saves there. Slowest shard, mean of 3: 701 s with, 702 s without. First shard start to the end of `Kit probes`: 711 s with, 712 s without.
3. **The gain is the tail**: the install step this removes was over 300 s in 43 of 205 executions in this workflow's history (21 %), over 15 minutes in 16 (8 %), and 1,963 s (32.7 minutes) at its worst successful run; p90 753 s. Since the split it hits five VMs at once (run 44808648029: two of five shards in one stalled install, 770 and 776 s). The image has no step that can stall like that. What replaces it, the pull, took 24 to 46 s in 15 of 15 jobs here (41.6 s in I1's one, 19 s p50 in 28 jobs for the old image); a pull that hangs is bounded only by the job's `timeout-minutes` (now 20): none was seen, and 16 pulls of this image are too few to say how often one would.
4. **Recommendation: keep the image in this job.** It is faster at the median, equal on wall, removes the longest tail the job had, and every probe passes in the container with the same seconds and the same output (below).

## Findings

- **The pnpm store cache is worth about nothing in this job.** All 15 image shard jobs missed it (the store path differs in the container, so the cache `version` is another one: it has to be primed once on main, which the push run after the merge does; the pull request's first runs miss) and `pnpm install` took 3.5 s cold against 1.3 s warm. Restoring costs 1 to 4 s and the save attempts 2 to 7 s in every shard; five 183 MB entries per key when it misses, in a repository cache that is already over its limit (I1/plan). I left it, because dropping it is not this step's; C1 (cache work) should drop it from this job.
- **A dispatch of the same ref cancels the run in progress**: the workflow's `concurrency` group is `ui-next-kit-probes-<pr number or ref>` with `cancel-in-progress`, so two `workflow_dispatch` runs of one branch cancel one another (I lost three runs to it before giving each run its own `wip/` ref). Not a defect for pull requests; a trap for measurement.
- `actions/cache` and `upload-artifact` work in the container with `${{ runner.temp }}` (the runner translates it); `chown` of `$PWD` is needed and enough (`git` ran in all steps); `/ms-playwright` is found through the image's `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH`; no `--shm-size` was needed for Chromium or WebKit.
- The first run of this workflow after the merge has a cold pnpm cache and pulls the image: a few seconds more.
- The docs `development-native-toolchain.md` and `development-workspaces.md` and the other four consumers (B2 to B4) are not this step's.

## The Vite test: a product race, left for the lead

The failure is always `vite_readiness_test.go:127: An error is expected but got nil. TERM-ignoring Vite descendant must not retain the listener after its pnpm leader exits` (54 of 54 at 0.11 to 0.28 s): the probe of the port succeeds after `vp.done` is closed.

`viteProcess.signalOwnedTree(true)` (`vite.go`, on context cancellation) and `waitForExit` send SIGKILL to the group and then `cmd.Wait()` the leader and `close(vp.done)`. Delivery to the descendant is asynchronous to the leader's: nothing waits until the descendant has gone. A temporary test (not committed) cancelled the context, waited for `done` and probed every 200 us: 5 of 300 runs under 8 loops still answered after `done` (1, 1, 1, 2 and 3 probes; the rest answered nothing). `Stop()` returns on `done`, and the manager releases the port (`ReleaseByPlugin`, `manager.go`) right after `inst.Stop()` returns, so the next start on the same port can meet the dying descendant's listener for some milliseconds. A test-side wait (`require.Eventually` for the listener to go) would hide that window; the fix is in the product: close `done` (or return from `Stop`) only after the group is empty. Reproduce: `go test -race -c ./backend/pkg/plugin/devserver`, then the binary with `-test.run '^TestViteContextCancellationKillsListeningDescendantBeforeReapingLeader$' -test.count=200` beside 8 busy loops.

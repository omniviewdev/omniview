## Finding

The traffic lights sit about 4 px above the centre of the new shell's 40 px top row (3 px above the old header's 38 px).

## Cause

`main.go` (`Mac.TitleBar: application.MacTitleBarHidden`, Wails `webview_window_options.go:833-840`) keeps AppKit's standard 28 pt title bar, so AppKit lays the lights out for it: disc centres at y = 15.5 to 16 from the window top. The new shell's top row is 40 px (`ui-next/shell/TopRow.module.css:14`, `Shell.module.css:9`), its tab centre is at y 19.5 to 20. The old header is 38 px (`ui/layouts/core/main/WorkspaceShell.module.css:6`), centre 19. CSS cannot move the lights; Wails 3.1.0 has no inset option.

## Change

`macTitleBar()` in `window_options.go`: `MacTitleBarHiddenInsetUnified` with `ToolbarStyle = MacToolbarStyleUnifiedCompact`, used at `main.go` for the window template. An empty compact unified toolbar makes AppKit lay the lights out for a 38 pt bar. Test: `TestMacTitleBarCentresTrafficLightsOnTheTopRow`. The change applies to every window made from the template (main and secondary); the Settings window overrides `Mac.TitleBar` itself (`backend/window/wails.go:76`) and is unchanged.

Finding (new UI, both before and after this change, not caused by it): the shell draws its three grey stand-in dots in the 84 px safe area (`ui-next/shell/TopRow.tsx:18-19`, `TopRow.module.css:29-52`, pinned by the shell probes) under the real lights. They peek out around the native lights, and since the lights moved 4 px right and 4 px down they now peek out to the left (about 3 px) instead of under them (compare `new-ui-before.png`, `new-ui-after.png`). In full screen, where the native lights are hidden, they are the only thing drawn there. Fix belongs to the shell owner (hide the stand-ins when running natively); I left them: the probes pin them.

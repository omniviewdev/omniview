## What is built

- **The bell** (`notices/Bell.tsx`, `Bell.module.css`; mounted by `StatusBar.tsx`, the only change to the frame): last in the status bar, 24 px, 16 px glyph, a 6 px dot of the worst unread severity, never a number (S81, A12). Its name and tooltip carry the count in words ("Notifications, 3 unread, 1 error", the model's `Bell.label`). `aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` while open. It is drawn only in a window whose `NoticesBindings.centre` exists.
- **The centre** (`Centre.tsx`, `CentreRow.tsx`, `FilterMenu.tsx`, `Centre.module.css`): a non-modal dialog in the top layer (`popover="manual"`, placed above the bell by `centreDom.placeAbove`, the mock's `placePopover`), drawn ONLY while open. Header; the bar (the kit's roving group: Mark all as read, Clear finished or Undo, Filter); a note under it for the source the list is narrowed to and for muted sources (Unmute, Unmute all); the list under Recent and a heading per day (Today, Yesterday, a date); rows 40 px collapsed with severity glyph and word, title, one line, the kit's `compactAge` time with `ageTip` in its tooltip, the unread dot (and the word Unread); a deduped repeat shows its count (the model's words: "Timed out 2 times", "Happened 3 times · ..."); the first 50 rows and "Show N older"; empty states (none, cleared with Undo, nothing matches). A row opens on its title (reads it at once) and then shows the record's actions that CAN be used and, for one that cannot, no button and the model's reason sentence ("Retry is unavailable. Not available after restart."): never a dead button. Row Dismiss removes the record (Undo dismiss for ten seconds), Delete does the same from the keyboard.
- **The rule of S81** (as the mock's `centerClosed`): opening keeps every row as it was; closing after at least one second marks what the centre LISTED as read, each record counted from the moment it was listed (`store.seen`); a row opened, acted on or dismissed is read at once; Mark all as read. While the centre is open the toast stack waits (`hold('center')`, S89a) and comes back, if its records were not read, when it closes.
- **Keyboard**: the bell is a Tab stop and opens on Enter and Space; focus goes to the newest row's title (the popover itself when there is no row); the list is ONE stop (`useRows`: only the current row's buttons are in the tab order, arrows, Home, End between rows, Delete removes); Escape closes while the focus is in it and the focus goes back to the bell; a press outside closes it and leaves the focus with what was pressed; the Filter menu (own, `role="menu"`, radio items, arrows, Home, End, Escape closes only the menu, Tab leaves it) is a nested popover. The mock binds NO chord to `host.notifications.open`, so none is built.
- **Filter and mute**: the mock's mute lives in the Filter menu (choose a source, then "Mute {name} for 1 hour" or "Unmute"), so the menu has the Source group, that item and Clear filters. The chosen source survives a close, as the mock's does.
- **Summary toast's Show now opens the centre.** O1b-1's deviation (the summary toast had no Show because the window had no centre) is REMOVED: `NoticesBindings.centre` is no longer a flag but the centre controller (`centreControl.ts`), the host makes it with the store and passes its `open` as the model's `openCenter`. The test that pinned "no centre yet" (`plugins/host/notices.test.ts`) now pins the centre, the Show and the palette closing it.
- **Persistence** (`notices/storage.ts`): the history is saved in a slot of its own, `notices`, in the host's window service, one burst at a time (the model's `serialise`, the model's saver, 400 ms, flush on `pagehide`/hidden), read before the window draws beside the session (`PluginHostScreen`), restored through `restoreNotices` (versioned, repaired not discarded). The session's promises hold: a text that is not JSON or is a newer version is KEPT ASIDE as `notices.invalid.<time>` and never overwritten; a place that cannot be read or does not answer in 2 s starts empty and saves nothing; writes go one at a time, in order. Restored records raise no toast and no announcement (the model's rule).
- **Host wiring** (minimal): `plugins/host/notices.ts` (centre, `openCenter`, `initial`, the saver in `attach`, the centre closes when the palette opens), `PluginHost.tsx` (one prop `savedNotices`, passed to `createHost`), `PluginHostScreen.tsx` (loads the history with the session).
- **Six kit glyphs** (approved by the lead): `bell`, `bell-off`, `check-check`, `trash`, `undo`, `list-filter` (CentreGlyphs; the patch is applied as is: `glyphs.ts`, tests, `Icon.examples.tsx`, `kit.api.md`, the kit probe's glyph comment). ## Differences from the mock (each is a decision; the lead can overrule)

1. **Not built, because the step is the history and not the views of it**: the segmented view (All, Unread, Active, Needs attention), the severity group of the Filter menu, a record's details (diagnostics, Copy: owner decision B6 is pending), the Active group and its progress (operations), the footer's two links (Open Operations, Notification settings): none has anything to open yet. The comparison with the mock therefore has no `centreSeg` and no `centreFooter`, and the list body is 33 px taller than the mock's (no footer); the mock's bar is 28 + 8 px taller (the view control).
2. **Time is the kit's** (`compactAge`: `5m`, `3h`, `2d`), not the mock's `ago()` ("5m ago", "now"); the tip is the kit's `ageTip` (UTC and words), kept as current as the label by the page's one age clock (`tipAge`).
3. **Severity is a word before the title** (visually hidden, as the toast's) instead of an `aria-label` on the article.
4. **Headings are real headings** (h4 Recent, h5 for each day) in `li role="presentation"`, where the mock's are `li` section labels.
5. **Expanded rows are not kept across a close** (the mock keeps them in its state); the source filter is.
6. **The popover is `popover="manual"` with its own light dismiss** (a press outside, Escape while the focus is in it), not `popover="auto"`: a press on the bell would otherwise close it on the pointer down and open it again on the click.
7. **Undo's label is by count** ("Undo dismiss" for one record, "Undo clear" for more), where the mock's follows the command.
8. **The centre closes when the palette opens** (a modal owns the window; the browser would do the same to an `auto` popover).

## Seam: the lead's sequence needs a decision

"failing page command → toast → dismissed → it is in the centre, unread count 1 → open → read" cannot hold as written: **dismissing a toast marks its record read** (the model, `dismiss`; the mock, `shell-notify.js:631`; S81), so after a Dismiss the bell has no unread. The seam assertion (not written) should prove: (a) toast dismissed, the record is in the centre, read, bell without a dot; (b) a second failure whose toast is NOT dismissed: the bell says "1 unread, 1 error", opening the centre takes the toast away (it is in the list, unread), closing it after more than a second reads it; (c) a reload of the page: the history is back (read), its Retry is not a button and its row says why. Persistence in the seam needs the 400 ms save and the window service (`SaveState`) to be awaited before the reload.
